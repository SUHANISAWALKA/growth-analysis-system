E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset to evaluate revenue performance, customer behavior, geographic trends, and customer segmentation using the RFM framework.
The objective is to generate actionable business insights that can improve customer retention, revenue growth, and long-term profitability.
Business Objectives
Analyze overall revenue performance
Measure customer acquisition vs retention
Identify top-performing states and product categories
Evaluate revenue concentration risk
Segment customers using RFM analysis
Provide strategic business recommendations
Key Metrics Analyzed
Total Revenue: 15.4M
Total Orders: 96,477
Total Customers: 93,357
Average Order Value (AOV): 159.86
Repeat Customer Rate: Low (majority one-time buyers)
Key Insights
1️⃣ Revenue Growth
Monthly revenue trend shows consistent growth, indicating strong customer acquisition and scaling operations.
2️⃣ Geographic Revenue Concentration
~37% of total revenue comes from a single state (SP), indicating geographic dependency risk.
3️⃣ Product Portfolio Performance
Top revenue-generating categories include:
Home & Living
Beauty & Health
Tech Accessories
These categories drive the majority of revenue.
4️⃣ Customer Retention Challenge
Orders ≈ Customers
Low repeat purchase behavior
Business heavily dependent on acquisition
5️⃣ RFM Segmentation
Customers were segmented based on:
Recency
Frequency
Monetary value
Findings indicate a large share of customers fall into low-frequency and at-risk segments.
